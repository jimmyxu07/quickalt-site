About QuickAlt

QuickAlt is a fast, free, and privacy-focused AI alt text generator. Our mission is to make the web more accessible by helping creators, developers, and marketers write better image descriptions in seconds.

Why we built QuickAlt

Writing alt text for every image is tedious, but skipping it hurts accessibility and SEO. We built QuickAlt to remove that friction with a tool that is anonymous, instant, and requires zero setup.

Whether you manage a blog, an online store, or a media-rich app, QuickAlt gives you descriptive alt text you can copy and use immediately.

Our values

  • Privacy first: We do not store uploaded images.
  • Accessibility first: Good alt text means a more inclusive web.
  • Simplicity: No accounts, no paywalls, no clutter.
