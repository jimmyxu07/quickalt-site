Frequently Asked Questions

What is alt text? expand_more

Alt text (alternative text) is a written description of an image used by screen readers and search engines to understand visual content. It improves accessibility and SEO.

Is QuickAlt free to use? expand_more

Yes. QuickAlt is free for everyday use. We may introduce fair usage limits to keep the service available for everyone.

Do you store my images? expand_more

No. We do not store uploaded images. They are processed temporarily to generate alt text and discarded immediately.

What image formats are supported? expand_more

QuickAlt supports PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP, and GIF files up to 10MB per image.
