Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

QuickAlt ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when using our website at quickalt.site.

Information we collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate the service:

  • Usage analytics (page views, button interactions) via Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics tool.
  • Technical data such as browser type and IP address may be processed transiently by our hosting provider and any API proxy used for image analysis.

Images and generated content

We do not store your images. Images uploaded to QuickAlt are processed in memory solely to generate alt text and are not retained on our servers after the request completes.

How we use information

We use collected information to improve site performance, understand usage patterns, and prevent abuse. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Third-party services

We may rely on third-party AI providers to analyze images and generate alt text. These providers process image data according to their own privacy policies and are only engaged for the duration of the generation request.

Cookies

QuickAlt does not use tracking cookies. Plausible analytics operates without cookies.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because we do not retain uploaded images or account data, there is typically no personal data stored by QuickAlt to request deletion.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the channels provided on this website.
